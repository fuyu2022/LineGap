LINE GAP {VERSION} — Windows x64

Extract the archive and launch LineGap.exe. Godot does not need to be installed.
Keep LineGap.pck in the same folder as the executable.

WASD / Arrow keys: Move
Left mouse button: Dash toward the clicked position
Space: Dash / Start / Restart / Resume
Esc: Pause / Resume
R: Restart

Language follows your system language on first launch (Chinese or English).
Click English / 中文 in the bottom-right corner to switch. Your choice is saved.

Collect gold orbs to gain points, refill your dash and gain lives (up to three).
Stage 1: Dashing temporarily phases obstacles.
Stage 2: Dashing shatters obstacles; dash cooldown is halved.
Stage 3: Dash distance is increased.
Taking damage grants two seconds of invulnerability. Best scores are saved automatically.

Engine and third-party notices are in Godot-Licenses.txt.
