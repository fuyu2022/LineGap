LINE GAP {VERSION} — Windows x64

Extract the archive and launch LineGap.exe. Godot does not need to be installed.
Keep LineGap.pck in the same folder as the executable.

WASD / Arrow keys: Move
Left mouse button: Dash toward the clicked position
Space: Dash / Start / Restart / Resume
Esc: Pause / Resume
R: Restart

Language follows your system language on first launch (Chinese or English).
Open Settings in the bottom-right corner, then click English / 中文 to switch. Your choice is saved.

Collect gold orbs to gain points, refill your dash and gain lives (up to three).
Stage 1: Dashing temporarily phases obstacles.
Stage 2: Dashing shatters obstacles; dash cooldown is halved.
Stage 3: Dash distance is increased.
Each shattered barrier awards 15 points.
Taking damage grants two seconds of invulnerability. Best score and most barriers shattered in one run are saved separately and shown at the top right.
Records display as score / barriers shattered / survival time (one decimal
place). Each value is its own historical maximum.

Every seven orbs open a seven-second super-dash window: no dash cooldown and
full invulnerability, including when not dashing. Press Space or left-click
to dash manually; movement speed is 2 times normal during this window.
Normal movement speed returns when the window ends. Contacting any barrier
shatters it, including while walking or standing still. Bursts can turn.
The dash bar and trail turn gold. Finishing grants the usual
two-second collision protection. Pause freezes all timers; restarting clears
the progress. Normal cooldown returns after the window ends.

Engine and third-party notices are in Godot-Licenses.txt.

Music mode: Click Music mode in the bottom-right corner (gold means enabled).
Switching modes restarts the current run. Space / left-click now releases a
wave without moving the player. On-beat waves phase every obstacle briefly;
only phased obstacles shatter when touched by a wave. The wave button turns
gray after use and resets on the next beat. Stages 1 / 2 / 3 increase its range.
Every seven orbs grant seven seconds of resonance: obstacles remain phased
and automatically shatter on each beat. Pause also pauses the music in this
mode. Muting keeps the beat clock running. Each mode has separate records.
On-beat judgement allows 230ms before or after a beat. While gray, a press up
to 80ms before the next available beat is buffered once and fires on that beat.
Repeated presses do not stack. Pause, focus loss, restart and death clear it.

Music: Open Settings and click Music: On / Off to toggle background
music only. Sound effects remain enabled. Music is on by default and your
choice is saved. Turning it back on resumes playback; pausing or restarting
a run does not restart the track.
